# Getting Started

Use Crucible to create and work in the lab VMs assigned to you.

## Sign in and open a lab

1. Sign in through Authentik and complete your multi-factor authentication prompt.
2. Open the Crucible Dashboard. Your existing labs appear under **My Labs**.
3. To start a new lab, choose a template or blueprint that your instructor has made available, give the lab a clear name, and select **Deploy**.
4. Wait for the deployment status to become active, then open the lab from **My Labs**.

**Expected result:** You can see your lab, its VMs, their status, and the details needed to connect.

## Choose the right next step

- Use [Creating and Managing Labs](labs.md) to deploy, power, extend, or remove a lab.
- Use [Connecting to Your VM](connecting.md) to open a console or connect over your VPN.
- Use [Testing and Feedback](testing.md) when your lab includes assessments.

Only work with labs and VM details shown in your own Crucible account.
